OtoGenda Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 12, 2026

OtoGenda is a mobile planner application developed and published by Otogenda. This Privacy Policy explains how OtoGenda handles information when you use the app.

Local Storage

OtoGenda primarily stores planner data locally on the user's device. This may include tasks, templates, preferences, scheduling data, reminder settings, and profile display preferences.

Ads

OtoGenda may use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the free version of the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, diagnostics, and usage information according to Google's policies.

Google Play Billing

OtoGenda may use Google Play Billing for subscriptions and premium features. Payment processing is handled by Google Play. OtoGenda does not directly process or store payment card information.

Calendar Permission

OtoGenda may request calendar permission only to create, view, or manage calendar-related planner features selected by the user.

Reminder and Notification Permission

OtoGenda may request notification permission to show reminders and task alerts configured by the user.

Personal Data

OtoGenda does not sell personal data.

Third-Party Services

OtoGenda may use third-party services such as Google AdMob and Google Play Billing. These services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Data Security

OtoGenda is designed to keep most planner and scheduling data locally on the user's device whenever possible.

Children's Privacy

OtoGenda is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy-related questions about OtoGenda, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
